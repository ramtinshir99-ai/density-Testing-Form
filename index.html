Test data

Grey boxes are calculated. Wet density = DD × (1 + MC ÷ 100). Compaction = DD ÷ Proctor × 100. Compaction colour: green meets the minimum (up to 0.5% under rounds to it), yellow is 0.5% to 2% under, red is 2% or more under.

New tests copy the material, Proctor, Proctor type, Proctor sample number, minimum compaction and reference point from the last one.

Notes
